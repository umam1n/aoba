# Navigating UU PDP Compliance in HR Technology

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) marks a seismic shift in how enterprises must handle data. While most companies focus on consumer data, employee data is often the most sensitive information an organization holds.

## The Challenge for HR

Predictive analytics requires data. But under UU PDP, processing employee data for predictive modeling without explicit consent and clear boundaries is a massive liability.

### AOBA's Privacy-First Architecture

We built AOBA from the ground up to comply with UU PDP Chapter 4 (Data Subject Rights).

1. **Consent Management:** AOBA natively tracks granular consent for data processing. If an employee opts out of predictive modeling, their data is seamlessly excluded from the DNN training sets without breaking the platform for the rest of the organization.
2. **Audit Trails:** Every time an HRBP views a flight risk score or accesses a salary band, the event is logged. 
3. **Data Minimization:** We only process the exact signals necessary for our models. No arbitrary scraping. No "collect it all and figure it out later."

Compliance isn't a feature; it's the foundation.
