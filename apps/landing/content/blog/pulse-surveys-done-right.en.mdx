# Pulse Surveys: Stop Asking "How Are You?"

The annual engagement survey is a relic of the past. It takes 3 months to design, 1 month to administer, and 2 months to analyze. By the time leadership reviews the results, the data is half a year old.

Enter the Pulse Survey. But there's a problem: most companies use pulse surveys to ask variations of "How are you feeling today?" 

## The Problem with Sentiment-Only Surveys

Sentiment is a lagging indicator of structural problems, and it's highly volatile. A bad commute can ruin a Tuesday survey score. 

### Asking the Right Questions

To predict attrition, pulse surveys must be tied to structural risk factors. Instead of asking about generic happiness, AOBA's built-in pulse engine asks targeted questions mapped to our core risk signals:

- **Role Clarity:** "In the last 30 days, have your core responsibilities changed without a formal update?"
- **Manager Support:** "Did you receive actionable feedback in your last 1-on-1?"
- **Growth Outlook:** "Do you see a clear path for advancement in your current department?"

By correlating these specific answers with our baseline structural data, we generate predictive insights that are actually actionable.
