# Predicting Attrition with Deep Neural Networks

Attrition is a complex, multi-variate problem. Rule-based systems are great for capturing obvious correlations (like low salary + long tenure = high risk), but they fail to capture the subtle, non-linear relationships that drive human behavior.

This is why AOBA uses a dual-engine architecture.

## The Dual-Engine Architecture

1. **Rule-Based Baseline:** We start with structural HR signals (compensation bands, tenure, leave usage) to establish a baseline risk score. This provides 100% explainability.
2. **DNN Refinement:** Our Deep Neural Network analyzes sequential data (like the frequency of manager changes, the historical trajectory of peer departures, and sentiment shifts in pulse surveys) to uncover hidden patterns.

### Why Explainability Matters in HR

A black-box model that says "Employee X has a 90% risk of leaving" is useless to an HRBP. They need to know *why* so they can take action. 

AOBA's DNN utilizes SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values to translate complex neural network outputs into human-readable factors. The result? A dashboard that not only predicts flight risk, but tells the HRBP exactly which levers to pull to prevent it.
