# The Danger of the Tenured Cliff

In traditional HR, long tenure is viewed as a sign of stability. If an employee has been with the company for 3 years, they are often marked as "safe" in succession planning documents.

Our AI models have found the exact opposite to be true.

## The 36-Month Itch

When we plotted the attrition probabilities of 50,000 corporate employees in Indonesia, we discovered a consistent pattern: the "Tenure Cliff". 

The probability of departure does not increase linearly. It spikes dramatically at specific intervals:
- **18 Months:** The "Is this right for me?" cliff.
- **36 Months:** The "I'm ready for a promotion, or I'm leaving" cliff.
- **48 Months:** The "My equity has vested, time to see what's out there" cliff.

### Why This Matters for HRBPs

If you are wait to check in on an employee *after* they hit the 36-month mark, you are already fighting a retention battle you didn't know started. 

By utilizing structural HR intelligence, AOBA automatically flags employees approaching these critical cliffs and correlates them with other risk signals (like stagnant compensation) to trigger proactive interventions.

Stop assuming tenure equals loyalty. Start tracking the cliffs.
